# Authors and provenance

- **Gomba et al. (2016)** — split-spectrum ionospheric correction method.
- **Yajun Zhang** — subsequent script development.
- **Xin Wang** — final NISAR LA/LB workflow update and integration, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), Hefei, China.

The workflow uses external software including GMTSAR, GMT, SNAPHU, NumPy,
SciPy, and netCDF4. Their authors and licenses remain independent from this
repository.

This repository intentionally has no `LICENSE` file until ownership and
redistribution permission for every derived component are confirmed.
